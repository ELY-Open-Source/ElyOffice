# 0.1.11 2026-10-23

## English

### Platforms
- Linux packages now build in AlmaLinux 8 and install on Debian 11, Ubuntu 20.04 and newer (they needed glibc 2.43, #34).
- Intel Macs no longer crash at launch (#31).

### New
- Every app's left and right panels fold: Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+S for the left, Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+B for the right, also in the View menu; the choice stays after a restart (#27).
- Settings choose the interface font and text size; ElyDoc, ElyTable, ElyShow and ElyPDF take a default zoom for new documents (#26).
- The print dialog presets two-sided printing (Settings › Documents), on all three platforms.
- ElyMark: body text in any system font (View › Other Font…), with digits of one width (#29); the status bar counts words, characters and the selection (#30); a right-click removes one file from the recent list (#28); line numbers by default in source mode, and the caret's line and column in the status bar (#23).
- ElyPDF: zoom buttons in the status bar; a zoom keeps the spot you were reading; drag thumbnails to reorder pages, with a line where they will land.
- ElyMail follows the system proxy (HTTP, SOCKS5), so sign-in works through a proxy (#35).
- The clipboard works natively on Wayland, without Xwayland (#24).

### Changes
- Gmail signs in with an app password (#35). Remove a Gmail account added with Google sign-in in 0.1.10 and add it again.
- When Outlook.com refuses IMAP (“authenticated but not connected”), ElyMail says what to do: sign in once at outlook.live.com and finish its check (#35).

### Fixes
- ElyMail: garbled Chinese folder names from 163 and similar servers (#32); a side-bar menu that opened by itself when the window came back; the divider between the list and the reading pane returning to the middle.
- ElyTable: text selection and the caret in the formula bar (#21).
- ElyPDF: a long bookmark list did not scroll.

## 中文

### 平台
- Linux 包改在 AlmaLinux 8 里构建，Debian 11、Ubuntu 20.04 及更新的发行版都能装（原要 glibc 2.43，#34）。
- Intel Mac 启动即崩已修（#31）。

### 新功能
- 所有应用的左右侧栏都可收起：Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+S 收左栏，Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+B 收右栏，“视图”菜单也有；重开照旧（#27）。
- 设置里可选界面字体与字号；ElyDoc、ElyTable、ElyShow、ElyPDF 可设新文档的默认缩放（#26）。
- 打印对话框预设双面打印（设置“文档”页），三端都有。
- ElyMark：正文可用任意系统字体（视图 › 其他字体…），数字等宽（#29）；状态栏显示字数、字符数与所选范围（#30）；右键可从最近文件里移除一项（#28）；源码模式默认显示行号，状态栏写出插入点的行列（#23）。
- ElyPDF：状态栏有缩放按钮；缩放后停在原来读到的位置；缩略图可拖动调整页序，落点有提示线。
- ElyMail：网络随系统代理（HTTP、SOCKS5），国内可经代理登录（#35）。
- Wayland 下剪贴板直接读写，不经 Xwayland（#24）。

### 变更
- Gmail 改用应用专用密码登录（#35）。0.1.10 里用 Google 登录添加的 Gmail 账户请移除后重新添加。
- Outlook.com 拒绝 IMAP（“authenticated but not connected”）时，ElyMail 写明处理办法：在 outlook.live.com 登录一次并完成验证（#35）。

### 修复
- ElyMail：163 等邮箱的中文文件夹名乱码（#32）；关闭再打开窗口，侧栏菜单自己弹出；列表与阅读区的分隔线重开后回到原位。
- ElyTable：公式栏里选择文字与插入点的位置（#21）。
- ElyPDF：书签多时列表滚不动。
