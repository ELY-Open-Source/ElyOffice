# Ely Office 0.1.12 update film

60 s, 1920×1080, 60 fps, 120 BPM (bar = 2 s). `index.html` is generated by `tools/scaffold.py`; do not edit it.

## Music

`tools/audio/cut.py` joins the 0.1.10 score: 0–28 s, then 58–90 s. A bar of section B, the tom fill, the F# chorus at 30, the unison hit at 48, the outro from 50, silence after 59.88.

Each feature scene grows from its ball with `FLOW.open` and folds into the next with `FLOW.close`, exact at the boundary. Captions live in `lib/captions.js`; keep content above y = 890.

## Rebuild

```sh
python tools/audio/build.py      # music.wav (cut) and sfx/*.wav
python tools/audio/mix.py        # mix.wav from music and scenes/*.cues.json
python tools/scaffold.py         # index.html
npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.184 render --fps 60 --quality delivery -o renders/ElyOffice-0.1.12-update.mp4
```
