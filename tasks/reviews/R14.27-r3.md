- apps/ely-tree/src/tools.rs:168 — The startup cutoff measures stderr-drain time, not the launcher's lifetime: `wait_with_output()` can wait for a descendant holding stderr after the launcher has exited. A launcher that starts a background helper and immediately fails (`sh -c "sleep 6 & echo 'no display' >&2; exit 1"`) therefore loses its startup error. With the unchanged extracted `settled` function, the launcher exited within 2 ms, but stderr stayed open for six seconds and `settled` returned `Ok(())` (`.work/R14.27/r3/stderr-direct.log`; independently confirmed in `stderr-timing.log`). This is an immediate failure inside the five-second window, not the documented late-failure limitation. Measure the launcher's exit separately from draining stderr, preserve its startup diagnostic, and add a regression where a descendant holds stderr beyond `STARTUP`.
